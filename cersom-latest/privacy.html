Sermon Scribe (“we”, “us”) is operated by [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME], [BUSINESS ADDRESS]. This policy explains what we collect, why, and who else sees it. You can reach us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and a securely hashed password. We never see or store your password in readable form.
  • The content you create. Sermon topics, scripture references, tone and length settings, any notes you enter, and the sermons generated for you.
  • Billing information. Your plan, subscription status, and billing period. We do not receive or store your card number. Card details go directly to Stripe and never touch our servers.
  • Basic technical data. IP address, browser type, and request timestamps, kept by our hosting provider for security and abuse prevention.

How your sermon content is processed

This is the part worth reading carefully. When you generate a sermon, the topic, scripture reference and any notes you provide are sent to Anthropic (the makers of Claude) so the sermon can be written. Anthropic processes that text on our behalf in order to return a result.

Do not enter confidential pastoral information, details of private counselling conversations, health information, or anything else told to you in confidence. Sermon Scribe is a drafting tool for material intended to be preached publicly, and it should be used that way.

Who else we share data with

We use a small number of providers, each for one purpose:

  • Anthropic — generates sermon text from your prompts.
  • Supabase — stores your account and your saved sermons.
  • Stripe — processes card payments and subscriptions.
  • Cloudflare — serves the website and protects it from abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Payments

Paid plans are billed in Canadian dollars through Stripe. Where Interac e-Transfer is offered as an alternative, we record that a claim was made and which plan it grants, so the payment can be matched to your account. Refer to Stripe’s own privacy policy for how they handle payment data.

How long we keep things

Account data and saved sermons are kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account we remove them within 30 days, except where we are required to keep billing records for tax and accounting purposes.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Canadian users have these rights under PIPEDA; users in the UK and EU have equivalent rights under the GDPR. Write to [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we will respond within 30 days.

You can delete your account at any time from your account settings, which removes your saved sermons along with it.

Children

Sermon Scribe is intended for adults preparing material for ministry. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and for anything significant we will email account holders before it takes effect.
